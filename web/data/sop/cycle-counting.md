# Cycle Counting (SOP WH-CC-01)

## Purpose
Cycle counts keep stock records accurate without closing the warehouse.

## Procedure
1. Choose the location to count from the count schedule or at the supervisor's request.
2. Count the visible stock at that location. Count blind: do not read the system quantity first.
3. Count each pallet or carton, and lift nothing above shoulder height without a ladder or forklift.
4. Enter the counted quantity. The system compares it with the expected quantity and shows the variance.

## Variances
- A variance of zero closes the count.
- Any variance must be recounted once by a second person.
- If the variance is confirmed, it is raised as an exception for the supervisor.
- Only a supervisor may approve a stock adjustment. Never adjust stock yourself.

## Photo counts
A photo of the shelf may be used to help count. The operator must confirm or correct the number before saving. Take the photo straight on, with the whole shelf in view and good light.
