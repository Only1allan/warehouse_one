# Damaged Goods Handling (SOP WH-QA-01)

## Purpose
This procedure explains what to do when stock is found damaged at any point: receiving, storage, picking or loading.

## Immediate steps
1. Stop handling the item. Do not return damaged inventory to its shelf.
2. Place the damaged inventory in the quarantine area (location QUAR) immediately.
3. Attach a red QUARANTINE tag to the item or pallet and write the SKU, quantity and date on the tag.
4. Complete form WH-QA-04 (Damaged Goods Report) before notifying the supervisor.
5. Notify the supervisor. Do not make a stock adjustment yourself.

## Form WH-QA-04
Record the SKU, quantity damaged, where and when the damage was found, a short description of the damage and a photo if possible. Sign and date the form.

## Decisions
Only a supervisor may decide to repair, return to supplier, downgrade or scrap quarantined stock. Quarantined stock must not be picked or dispatched.

## Hazardous damage
If a damaged item is leaking or hazardous, follow the Chemical Storage procedure first and use a spill kit before moving the item.
