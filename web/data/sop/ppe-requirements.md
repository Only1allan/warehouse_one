# Personal Protective Equipment by Zone (SOP WH-SF-01)

## Minimum PPE in all zones
Hi-vis vest and safety boots are required everywhere on the warehouse floor.

## Zone A (spare parts and small items)
Hi-vis vest, safety boots and work gloves. Safety goggles when using cutting tools.

## Zone B (chemicals and power equipment)
Hi-vis vest, safety boots, safety goggles and chemical-resistant gloves when handling chemicals. Ear defenders near running generators and pumps.

## Zone C (packaging)
Hi-vis vest, safety boots and cut-resistant gloves when handling strapping band or cutting wrap.

## Zone D (safety stock, retail goods and building materials)
Hi-vis vest, safety boots and a safety helmet. Gloves are required when handling roofing sheets, tiles or steel bars.

## Quarantine area
Hi-vis vest, safety boots, gloves and goggles. Treat all quarantined goods as potentially hazardous.

## Rules
PPE must be inspected before use and replaced when damaged. Report missing PPE to the supervisor. PPE is stocked in Zone D.
