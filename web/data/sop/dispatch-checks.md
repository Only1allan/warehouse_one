# Dispatch Verification (SOP WH-DS-01)

## Purpose
This procedure makes sure the right items, quantities and destination are on every truck.

## Before loading
1. Confirm the order number and the destination on the loading sheet.
2. Check that every line on the order has been picked.

## During loading
1. Load the items and count each line as it goes on the truck.
2. Compare the loaded quantity with the ordered quantity for each line.
3. If the loaded quantity is lower than ordered, do not close the truck. Tell the supervisor.

## Before the truck leaves
1. Check the load is secured with straps and nothing can fall.
2. The driver and the dispatch clerk sign form WH-DS-03 (Dispatch Checklist).
3. Confirm the dispatch in the system so stock is reduced.

## Shortfalls
A shortfall must be recorded as an exception. Never dispatch a partial order without supervisor approval.
