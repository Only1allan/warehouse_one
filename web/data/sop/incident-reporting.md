# Incident and Near-Miss Reporting (SOP WH-IN-01)

## What to report
Report every injury, spill, fire, vehicle collision, near miss and equipment failure, however small.

## Steps
1. Make the area safe and help anyone who is hurt. For serious injury call the emergency number first.
2. Tell your supervisor immediately.
3. Complete incident form WH-IN-01 within 1 hour of the event.
4. Do not move equipment or goods involved in the incident until the supervisor allows it, unless needed for safety.

## Equipment issues
If equipment such as a forklift or conveyor needs maintenance, report it as an equipment issue and tag it out of service.

## Fire and evacuation
On the alarm, stop work, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point at the main gate. Do not use lifts. Do not go back inside until the supervisor says it is safe.
