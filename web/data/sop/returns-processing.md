# Returns Processing (SOP WH-RT-01)

## Purpose
This procedure explains how to process goods returned by customers.

## Steps
1. Check that the customer has a return authorisation number. Do not accept returns without one.
2. Inspect the goods against the return authorisation. Check quantity and condition.
3. Complete form WH-RT-07 (Return Inspection) for every return.
4. Sort returned goods into three groups: resaleable, damaged and wrong item.
5. Resaleable goods go back to stock after the supervisor signs form WH-RT-07.
6. Damaged goods go to the quarantine area and follow the Damaged Goods Handling procedure.
7. Wrong items are held in the returns bay for collection.

## Time limit
Returns must be inspected within 24 hours of arrival.
