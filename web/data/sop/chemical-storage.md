# Chemical Storage and Spill Response (SOP WH-SF-02)

## Storage rules
1. Store chemicals only in Zone B racks 1 and 2 (bunded shelves).
2. Keep acids, alkalis, solvents and oils apart from each other. Never store incompatible chemicals on the same shelf.
3. Store heavy containers on the lowest shelf. Never store liquid containers above shoulder height.
4. Keep every container closed and labelled. Do not decant into unmarked containers.
5. Keep the safety data sheet (SDS) for each product at the chemical store entrance.
6. No food, drink or smoking within 10 metres of the chemical store.

## Personal protective equipment
Wear chemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles and a chemical apron when handling open containers.

## Spill response
1. Warn people nearby and stop work in the area.
2. Put on gloves and goggles.
3. Contain the spill with spill absorbent pads from the spill kit. Pads are stocked as SKU 400430.
4. Place used pads in the marked hazardous waste bin.
5. Report the spill on incident form WH-IN-01 within 1 hour.
