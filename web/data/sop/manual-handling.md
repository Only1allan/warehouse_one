# Manual Handling and Lifting (SOP WH-SF-03)

## Safe lifting limits
- A single person must not lift more than 20 kg.
- Loads between 20 kg and 40 kg need a two-person lift.
- Loads over 40 kg must be moved with a pallet jack, trolley or forklift.

## Technique
1. Plan the lift and check the route is clear.
2. Stand close to the load with feet apart and bend your knees, not your back.
3. Keep the load close to your body and avoid twisting.
4. Put the load down carefully.

## Stacking
Do not stack cartons higher than 1.5 metres by hand. Stack heavy items at the bottom. Never block aisles, fire exits or extinguishers.
