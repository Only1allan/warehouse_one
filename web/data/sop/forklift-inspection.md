# Forklift Daily Inspection (SOP WH-EQ-01)

## Purpose
Every forklift must be inspected before use at the start of each shift. Only trained and authorised operators may drive forklifts.

## Pre-use checklist
1. Check tyres for damage and correct pressure.
2. Check forks for cracks, bends and wear. Check the fork pads (SKU A341).
3. Check the mast and chains for leaks, damage and smooth movement.
4. Check hydraulic hoses and fluid level for leaks.
5. Test the horn, lights and reversing alarm.
6. Test the brakes and the parking brake.
7. Check the seat belt and the operator restraint.
8. Check the battery or fuel level and the charging connections.
9. Check the load plate is present and readable.
10. Record the inspection on form WH-EQ-02.

## If a fault is found
Tag the forklift out of service with a red tag and do not use it. Report the fault as an equipment issue so maintenance and the supervisor are told.
