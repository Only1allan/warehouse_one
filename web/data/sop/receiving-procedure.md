# Receiving Procedure (SOP WH-RC-01)

## Purpose
This procedure makes sure every delivery is checked against its purchase order before stock is accepted.

## Before the truck arrives
1. Check the receiving schedule for expected purchase orders (POs) and suppliers.
2. Clear the receiving bay and make sure a pallet jack and scanner are available.

## Checking a delivery
1. Ask the driver for the delivery note and confirm the PO number on the note matches the schedule.
2. Inspect the load for visible damage before signing. Note any damage on the delivery note.
3. Count every line physically. Do not trust the quantity printed on the delivery note.
4. Compare the counted quantity with the PO quantity for each line.

## Discrepancies
- Short delivery: record the missing quantity and tell the supervisor.
- Over delivery: do not accept extra quantity until the supervisor approves.
- Wrong item: place it aside, record it as unexpected and tell the supervisor.
- Damaged item: follow the Damaged Goods Handling procedure.
Record every discrepancy on form WH-RC-02 (Receiving Discrepancy Report).

## Completing the receipt
Confirm the receipt in the system. Stock is added to the product's default location. Put away the goods within 2 hours of receipt.
